About Me

I am a Computer Vision Researcher with a B.E. in Computer Science from Yanshan University (2025, GPA 80.36/100, Top 33%), currently working as a Computer Vision Engineer at Tiandy Technologies while pursuing self-directed research toward Ph.D. opportunities. My research interests lie at the intersection of edge AI deployment, lightweight neural networks, and hyperspectral image analysis — building models that are accurate under limited supervision and efficient enough to run on real devices.

I am currently independently reproducing and extending the S2DL method (Cui et al., IEEE TGRS 2024) for unsupervised hyperspectral image clustering — a line of work I plan to continue at the doctoral level. My undergraduate edge-AI project, a 245-class mobile garbage classifier, is fully open-sourced on GitHub.

Looking for: Ph.D. / Research Assistant positions in computer vision, with a focus on annotation-efficient learning, hyperspectral and remote-sensing analysis, and edge deployment. See my research proposal outline below.

Doctoral Research Direction (Proposal Outline)

Annotation-Efficient and Edge-Deployable Learning for Hyperspectral Clustering and Small-Object Recognition in Remote Sensing and Medical Imaging. Building directly on my reproduction of S2DL, the proposed work investigates: (1) geometry-aware, label-efficient hyperspectral clustering; (2) small-object detection/segmentation under limited annotations; and (3) deployment-aware compression that preserves small and subtle targets. Target venues: TGRS, MICCAI, ISBI, ICMR, IGARSS.
